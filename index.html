About Me

My path into software engineering wasn't traditional, but it taught me exactly what it takes to build resilient software. In 2023, I was awarded a scholarship by African Leadership University (ALU). It was a massive opportunity, but it came with significant hurdles. Resources were incredibly scarce, internet connectivity was a constant battle, and electricity wasn't always guaranteed.While those constraints would have stopped many, they became my ultimate training ground.This journey transformed me from someone just learning to code into a highly resourceful and adaptable engineer. I don't just want to build applications; My desire is to design systems engineered to survive constraints and solve real-world problems. Today, I am focused on creating clean, efficient, and impactful software architectures. When I am not writing code, I would want to be looking for new engineering challenges to tackle.

Refusing to give up, I searched for online courses to build my foundational knowledge from scratch. I enrolled in a web development course through Alison. While web development wasn't the full-scope software engineering I am studying now, it introduced me to the vital tools, logic, and resources I needed to get my foot in the door.Using that background knowledge, I took the next big step. My brother James, who shares the same passion for technology, referred me to a software engineering program at Media Crest College.Today, my goal stretches far beyond the classroom. I desire not just to learn theory, but to actively apply my engineering knowledge to solve real-world problems and build functional, impactful software.

I have a profound passion for growing and uplifting others. For me, technology is not just about writing clean logic or setting up servers; it is a tool for empowerment. My ultimate goal is to use my skills to build up the people and communities around me, ensuring that as the digital world moves forward, nobody gets left behind.This core belief drives me to push past every barrier. No matter how scarce resources may be, I am committed to fighting for the tools, the data, and the hardware needed to master my craft. I treat every constraint not as a permanent roadblock, but as a temporary puzzle that demands a creative solution.The ultimate reward is the ability to deploy robust, vital systems that can make a tangible difference in our fast-changing world. Bringing a complex application to life and watching it solve real-world problems is exactly why I write code. This is my passion. This is my pride.

Projects

  1. Prime Grow Farms Web Platform

    The Concept: A comprehensive digital platform for a modern, multi-sector agribusiness that incorporates poultry farming, beef cattle management, horticulture, and apiculture (bee keeping).

    Engineering Focus: Architecting a clean, content-rich user interface that handles multiple operational categories smoothly. It focuses on intuitive navigation so clients can easily explore different farming sectors, request quotes, or track farm-to-table supply chains.

  2. King Investment Holdings Portal

    The Concept: A secure corporate website for an investment holding firm specializing in pooled investing, where multiple investors combine capital for higher-yield opportunities.

    Engineering Focus: Emphasizing security, professional data presentation, and clear informational architecture. The system is structured to clearly explain pooled fund mechanics, display portfolio diversification, and provide secure communication channels for potential stakeholders.

  3. Digital Property Discovery & Locator System

    The Concept: A modern real estate application designed to completely eliminate the need for grueling, physical house hunting by allowing users to find and visually locate available homes remotely.

    Engineering Focus: Implementing search and filtering logic based on location, price, and amenities. It integrates mapping features (or coordinate-based tracking) to give users exact geographical context, managing dynamic UI state updates as users browse listings.

  4. Family Financial Management Ecosystem

    The Concept:A comprehensive personal finance application built to track and centralize an entire family's financial health, featuring modules for expenses, income streams, active debts, long-term investments, and liquid savings.

    Engineering Focus:Designing a unified Family Dashboard that aggregates complex, multi-source financial data into visual, easy-to-read charts. It relies on robust state management, strict mathematical accuracy for calculation logic, and responsive layouts for family members on different devices.
